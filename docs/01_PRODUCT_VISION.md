# 01 — Product Vision

## Product
BoxOp is a cross-platform spatial measurement and storage-placement application for iPhone and Android.

It turns the phone into a professional handheld tool for:
- measuring objects;
- measuring physical containers (Bins);
- measuring storage locations (Spots);
- saving digital records;
- checking physical compatibility;
- recommending and recording placement.

## Core workflow
`Measure → Validate → Save → Match → Assign → Export/Sync`

Example:
1. Measure a cardboard box (Object).
2. Save L × W × H, volume, photos, weight and notes.
3. Compare it with registered Spots and/or Bins.
4. Show compatible choices and required orientation.
5. Assign it.
6. Preserve the placement in history.

## Critical scope rule
BoxOp does **not** automatically map an entire warehouse.

Spatial mapping is optional, bounded and explicitly initiated by the user. A user may map a 5×5 ft station, one rack section, one room zone or another selected area.

Spots and Bins work even when no spatial map exists.

## Progressive hardware
The phone is the intelligence center. Premium depth sensors and external hardware improve capabilities but are not mandatory for the core MVP.

## Trust
Never present an estimate as certified/exact. Measurement quality and provenance are first-class data.

## Future
Packing optimization, pallets, trailers/containers, WMS/ERP, barcode/RFID workflows, Bluetooth scales, portable calibration stations and advanced occupancy intelligence.
